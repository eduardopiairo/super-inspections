# Super Inspections

A digital checklists and inspections platform inspired by [SafetyCulture](https://safetyculture.com). Super Inspections lets teams build, conduct, and track inspections from any device — turning paper-based processes into structured, auditable digital workflows.

## Features

- **Template Builder** — Create reusable inspection templates with sections, questions, and response types (yes/no, multiple choice, text, photo, signature, date)
- **Conduct Inspections** — Complete inspections on web or mobile with offline support
- **Actions & Follow-ups** — Raise corrective actions directly from failed items and assign them to team members
- **Reports & Analytics** — Auto-generate PDF reports and track inspection scores over time
- **Roles & Permissions** — Manage access by organization, team, and role
- **Audit Trail** — Every submission is timestamped and immutable
